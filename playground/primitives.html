Layout primitives

A layout is a tree of boxes. Each container lays its children out in one direction, with a gap between them, padding inside, and alignment on both axes. Each child is sized along each axis: grow into the leftover space, fit its content, a fixed size, or a percent of the container.

Keywords are data attributes (data-direction, data-align-x, data-w="grow"); amounts are custom properties (--child-gap, --padding, --w). Start and end follow the reading direction, so a right-to-left page mirrors with no extra CSS: try the Direction control in the Design panel.
